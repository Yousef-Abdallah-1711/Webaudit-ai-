| Aspect | v1 | v2 |

## D. Recovery limitation (explicit, as requested)

With the conservative redelivery rule, a unit whose host is lost in `ADMITTED` or `DISPATCHED` stays non-terminal until the plan-deadline sweep finalizes it: the bound is the plan's remaining deadline (up to the 6 h ceiling) plus one sweep interval. Its F07 lease expires within 30 s of the last renewal, so concurrency is freed far earlier than the status row is finalized, and the unit can show as in progress without a live host. **No shorter bound is claimed** while AR-19 (transactional unit-keyed release) and AR-22 (hosting-failure backstop and orphan reconciler) are unresolved. The same applies to a lost queue job and to a producer crash before enqueue.

## E. Decisions this review surfaces (owner input; defaults stay conservative)

- **OD-A** single-host ownership primitive at 009 level (R-2): (1) generic host-claim step before F07 in 009 (new record, 012's claim becomes its specialization); (2) every non-passive engine must declare an equivalent claim (012 already has one); (3) accept token-sharing exposure and rely on CAS plus lease fencing alone. Trade-off: (1) widest 009 change, strongest; (2) smallest 009 text, relies on each engine spec; (3) weakest — a stalled-job redelivery can yield two hosts with the same token before the CAS decides. Default until decided: v2 requires "sole hosting ownership before F07" without choosing the mechanism.
- **OD-C** reason/cause for "F07 lease lost before `RUNNING`" (R-7c): leave to sweep (default) vs add a closed reason in 009/012.
- **Approval of two proposed rules** that go beyond the owner's Q2 statements: the **start-gate** (R-1) and the **post-F07 deadline re-check** (R-8), and the **host loop from `PROCEED`** (R-3).
