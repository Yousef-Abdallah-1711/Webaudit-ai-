- **Addendum (post-review, independent adversarial pass)**: this spec's own mandatory independent
  adversarial review (run after `/speckit-analyze`, per the triggering brief's workflow) found a
  more severe gap than anything in this checklist's original pass: FR-004's ownership precondition
  checks the *Target*, not the *Scope*'s content, so nothing originally prevented a grant on a
  legitimately owned Target from naming an entirely unrelated, unverified host or repository in its

  `data-model.md`'s validation rules, and `grant-lifecycle-contract.md`'s `createAuthorization`
  preconditions (precondition 7) — every Scope entry must now be the owning Target's own canonical
  value or a subdomain/exact match of it, with no exception. This did not require re-running every
