## 4. Actual residual blast radius (not complete tenant isolation)

With FR-201 enforced, a compromised worker instance can: claim any live non-terminal unit of any tenant in its `servedClasses`; refuse it with any tier-C reason, or drive it to `RUNNING` and finish it with any outcome 009 allows; lease that tenant's credential through the broker within the R1–R3 rates and the R7 quota; and block the direct dependents of units it claimed. Without a claim it can only refuse `PLANNED` units of its classes for the four step-0 reasons whose predicates the database already holds true. It cannot call the sweep, touch another class, a terminal unit, another principal's claim, or forge a verified predicate. A sustained single-tenant burst can still quarantine a principal for every tenant (R3). Without FR-201 the residual is unbounded and activation is refused.
